Allow about 20 minutes: 5 minutes preparation and 15 minutes cooking.

1. **Sauté cumin and curry leaves.**

   Finely chop the onion, tomato and chillies; grate the tofu into fine shreds for a finer texture. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add cumin and curry leaves; stir until fragrant and the cumin starts to sizzle, about 30 seconds. Lower the heat if the seeds darken quickly.

2. **Add onion and chillies.**

   Stir them into the pan and cook until the onion turns soft and translucent, about 4–6 minutes. Stir often; with little oil, add a splash of water if it sticks. Keep cooking if the onion is still firm; it needn't brown.

3. **Add everything except coriander.**

   Add tomato, grated tofu, salt, garam masala, turmeric and nutritional yeast. Stir over medium-low heat until the tomato softens and the tofu is hot throughout, about 5–7 minutes. Add a splash of water if the spices catch; cook off excess water if it looks wet. Turn off the heat once it's a moist scramble.

4. **Top with coriander.**

   With the heat off, scatter generously with chopped coriander. Don't sauté it; keep it fresh on top. Serve straight away.
