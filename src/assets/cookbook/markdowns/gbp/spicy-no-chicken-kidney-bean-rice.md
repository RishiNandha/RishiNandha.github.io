Allow about 30 minutes: 5 minutes preparation and 25 minutes cooking, with rice and sauce cooked alongside each other.

1. **Cook the rice.**

   Rinse the rice. Add it to a saucepan with the measured water and salt to taste. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the grains expand and are tender, about 10 to 15 minutes. Drain any excess water. While the rice cooks, start the sauce in a separate pan.

2. **Coat the no-chicken in spices.**

   Put the no-chicken, chilli bean paste, garam masala and cumin in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Stir for 2 to 3 minutes until coated and fragrant. Add a splash of water if the paste catches. Use less chilli bean paste for milder heat.

3. **Add beans, tomatoes and seasonings.**

   Add the drained kidney beans, chopped tomatoes, Italian herbs, garlic granules and pepper. Simmer gently, stirring regularly, for about 15 to 20 minutes, until the sauce is thick and everything is piping hot. Follow the no-chicken pack's cooking instructions if they need longer. Taste before adding more salt.

4. **Serve with rice.**

   Turn off the heat while the sauce is still moist, before it starts sticking or burning at the bottom. Serve over the rice. The 1× recipe makes one serving; 2× uses a full no-chicken pack and full tins for two servings.
